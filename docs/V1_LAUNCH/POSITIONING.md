## What VelocityMaid IS

### Compliance Infrastructure
VelocityMaid is **governance-first infrastructure** for contractor-based businesses. We provide the systems, processes, and audit trails needed to collect, verify, and track compliance documentation at scale.

### Core Capabilities

**Document Collection & Verification**
- Self-service W-9 collection from contractors
- Admin verification workflows
- Secure storage with encryption
- TIN masking (no full exposure in UI)

**Readiness & Risk Management**
- 1099 readiness scoring
- Proactive blocker identification
- Countdown to compliance deadlines
- Year-over-year trend visibility

**Audit-Ready Artifacts**
- Investor-ready PDF summaries (non-sensitive)
- Board compliance reports
- Data room exports with cryptographic integrity
- Signed checksum manifests for diligence

**Operational Infrastructure**
- Role-based access control (admin vs. contractor)
- Status lifecycle management
- Contact message system
- Reply templates and internal notes
- Full conversation history

### Value Proposition

**For Operators:**
- Reduce compliance risk without replacing existing systems
- Clear visibility into contractor readiness
- Professional artifacts for investors and board
- Low-disruption adoption (runs in parallel)

**For Investors & Partners:**
- Transparent compliance posture
- Audit-ready documentation
- Cryptographic integrity verification
- Professional governance signals

## What VelocityMaid IS NOT

### ❌ Not Payroll
VelocityMaid does not process payroll, calculate wages, or handle tax withholdings. We are compliance infrastructure that runs **alongside** your existing payroll system.

**What this means:**
- You keep using your current payroll provider
- We collect and verify compliance documentation
- We track readiness, not payments
- Payments are optional and staged later

### ❌ Not Legal Advice
VelocityMaid is not a law firm, tax advisor, or legal counsel. We provide infrastructure to collect and track compliance documentation. You work with your legal and tax advisors for interpretation and advice.

**What this means:**
- We don't interpret tax law
- We don't provide legal opinions
- We don't guarantee compliance outcomes
- You remain responsible for compliance decisions

### ❌ Not Employer-of-Record
VelocityMaid does not act as an employer-of-record, co-employment service, or staffing agency. We are a software platform that helps you manage compliance documentation for your contractors.

**What this means:**
- We don't employ your contractors
- We don't handle employment relationships
- We don't provide EOR services
- You maintain direct contractor relationships

### ❌ Not a Replacement System
VelocityMaid is not designed to replace your existing payroll, HR, or payment systems. We are a **parallel compliance layer** that enhances governance without disrupting operations.

**What this means:**
- No migration required
- No system replacement
- Runs alongside existing workflows
- Adds compliance visibility, doesn't replace operations
