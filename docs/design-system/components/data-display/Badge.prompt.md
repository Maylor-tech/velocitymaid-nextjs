Pill label for status, category, or counts.

```jsx
<Badge variant="cyan">Most Popular</Badge>
<Badge variant="success" icon={<Check size={13} />}>Verified</Badge>
```

Variants: `neutral | navy | cyan | cyanSolid | success | warning | danger`. Sizes `sm | md`. Semantic tints are intentionally calm (no neon). For job lifecycle states use `StatusBadge` instead.
