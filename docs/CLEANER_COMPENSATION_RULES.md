# Cleaner compensation rules

Owner rules dated Nov 19, 2026.

- PERCENTAGE: cleaner gets <rate>% of the eligible cleaning-service amount. Eligible = agreed cleaning price only. Exclude tips (tips stay 100% the cleaner's, separate flow), reimbursements, processing allowance, travel fees and other add-ons unless the assignment says "included".
- HOURLY: verified hours × agreed hourly rate. Hours come from the app's start/finish times as a SUGGESTION only; an admin must confirm ("verified hours", who, when) before anything is payable.
- Dorothy defaults: solo = PERCENTAGE 65%; assisted (owner works alongside) = HOURLY $30. Future cleaners: terms set per cleaner, no default assumed.
- Compensation method says nothing about employee vs contractor; don't label it that way anywhere.
